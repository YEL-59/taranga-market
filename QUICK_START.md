# 🚀 Taranga Market - Quick Start Guide

## 📋 Quick Navigation

### Main Features
- 🏠 **Home** - Featured & recent listings
- 📂 **Categories** - Browse all items
- ❤️ **Favorites** - Your saved items

### Categories
- 🚗 Vehicles
- 🏘️ Properties  
- 📱 Products/Phones
- 🛠️ Services
- 💼 Jobs

---

## ⚡ Quick Actions

### Browse Items
1. Click a category card on homepage
2. OR click "Categories" in navigation
3. Scroll through items

### Filter Results
**Desktop**: Use sidebar on left
**Mobile**: Tap "Filters" button

### View Details
Click "View Details" button or item card

### Save Favorites
Click ❤️ heart icon on any item

---

## 🎯 Common Tasks

### Find a Car
1. Go to **Vehicles** category
2. Filter by: Make, Year, Price
3. View details of interesting cars
4. Save favorites with ❤️

### Find a Property
1. Go to **Properties** category
2. Filter by: City, Bedrooms, Price
3. Check amenities (WiFi, Pool, etc.)
4. Contact seller for viewing

### Find Electronics
1. Go to **Products** category
2. Filter by: Brand, Condition, Price
3. Check specifications
4. Compare multiple items

### Find Services
1. Go to **Services** category
2. Filter by: Service Type, City
3. Check ratings & experience
4. Contact for quote

### Find Jobs
1. Go to **Jobs** category
2. Filter by: Job Type, Remote, Industry
3. Check salary & requirements
4. Apply or contact employer
